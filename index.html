10 days that never happened

By 1582 the calendar had drifted 10 days away from the seasons. Pope Gregory XIII fixed it: Thursday 4 October was followed by Friday 15 October.

The leap-year hack

Earth’s year is 0.2422 day longer than 365. Over 4 years: 4 × 0.2422 = 0.9688, almost 1 whole day. So we add 29 February. Try any year.

Rule: divisible by 4 is a leap year, unless divisible by 100, unless also divisible by 400.

What to remember

Rotation makes a day (24 hours). The Moon makes a month (29.5 days). Revolution makes a year (365.2422 days).

Earth’s tilt, not its distance from the Sun, causes the seasons.

Leap years fix the extra quarter day. The 100 and 400 rules fix the rest, so the Gregorian calendar stays within one day for about 3,000 years.

Every date you write is a bet on the Sun.
